# When Witness Search Becomes Address Construction

A guarded bridge note between Stephen Wolfram's ruliological P-vs-NP microcosms and τ-admissible witness construction.

This Research Note reads Wolfram's January 2026 essay *"P vs NP and the Difficulty of Computation: A Ruliological Approach"* (DOI [10.31855/be91a2c3-b2c](https://doi.org/10.31855/be91a2c3-b2c)) as an external comparator for a separate and bounded internal question: under what finite-width discipline does witness search admit a constructive address-resolution alternative to blind enumeration? The two readings are explicitly parallel, not nested.

The note does not claim to solve orthodox P vs NP, does not identify the Ruliad with Category τ, and does not treat Wolfram's finite experiments as validation of Panta Rhei.
